Important Security Notice

Protect your passwords with these essential practices

🔒 Use App Lock on Your Device

Important: Always enable app lock, fingerprint, or face recognition on your phone or computer. This ensures that only YOU can access your saved passwords.

Tip: On Android, use App Lock. On iPhone, use Screen Time. On Windows, use BitLocker or PIN lock.

Never Share Your Passwords

Your passwords are for your eyes only. Never share them with anyone, not even friends or family.

Update Regularly

Change your passwords every 3-6 months for maximum security. Our generator makes it easy to create new ones.

Use 2FA When Available

Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security. Always enable it on important accounts.
